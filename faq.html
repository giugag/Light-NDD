About the study

Have a question? Browse the topics below to find out more about the study.

What is the Light_NDD study? +

Light_NDD is a research study exploring whether sensitivity to light plays a role in sleep, circadian rhythms and wellbeing in young adults aged 18–25 who are autistic, have ADHD, or both (AuDHD), compared with adults without a neurodevelopmental condition.

Who can take part? +

The study is open to young adults aged 18–25 who are autistic, have ADHD, or both (AuDHD), as well as adults without a neurodevelopmental condition. Additional eligibility criteria apply.

Do I need a formal diagnosis of autism or ADHD? +

No. Diagnosis or self-identification is accepted for autistic and ADHD participants.

What will I have to do if I take part? +

Participation involves wearing a wrist actiwatch and a light sensor for two weeks, completing online questionnaires, and attending an evening research session at Maynooth University where we measure how your pupils respond to light.

How long does participation take? +

The wearable monitoring period lasts two weeks. You will also complete questionnaires and attend an evening laboratory session. Please contact the study team for further details about the time commitment.

Will I receive anything for taking part? +

Participants will receive a €180 voucher and a graphical summary of their wearable data, in recognition of their time.

Can I withdraw from the study? +

Participation is voluntary. You will receive further information about your rights, including withdrawal, before deciding whether to take part. Please refer to the Participant Information Sheet for the applicable withdrawal arrangements.
