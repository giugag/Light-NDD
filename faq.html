About the study

Have a question? Browse the topics below to find out more about the study.

What is the Light_NDD study? +

Light_NDD is a research study exploring whether sensitivity to light plays a role in sleep, circadian rhythms and wellbeing in young adults aged 18–25 who are autistic, have ADHD, or both (AuDHD), compared with adults without a neurodevelopmental condition.

Who can take part? +

The study is open to young adults aged 18–25 who are autistic, have ADHD, or both (AuDHD), as well as adults without a neurodevelopmental condition. Please check the exclusion criteria to find out whether you are eligible to participate.

Do I need a formal diagnosis of autism or ADHD? +

No. Diagnosis or self-identification is accepted for autistic, ADHD, and AuDHD participants.

What will I have to do if I take part? +

Participation involves wearing a wrist actiwatch and a light sensor for two weeks, completing online questionnaires, and attending an evening in-lab session at Maynooth University where we measure how your pupils respond to light.

How long does participation take? +

The wearable monitoring period lasts two weeks. You will also complete questionnaires and attend an evening laboratory session.

Will I receive anything for taking part? +

Upon completion of all study procedures, participants will receive a €180 voucher and a graphical summary of their wearable data, in recognition of their time.

Can I withdraw from the study? +

Yes, participation is voluntary. You may choose to withdraw from this study at any time.

Can I participate if I wear glasses or contacts? +

Yes. However, lenses must be removed during the pupillometry session.

When can I remove the devices and where should I put them? +

You should remove both devices when showering and bathing, swimming, or engaging in any contact sports or activity that might make wearing the device dangerous.
The Actigraphy Watch should be worn at all other times, including during sleep. The ActLumus Light Sensor should be removed prior to sleep and placed on a bedside table with the sensor facing upwards.

Is the pendant recording video or audio like a camera? +

No. The neither the watch, nor the light sensor, can record audio or video. Although the light sensor may look like a camera, it can only record data relating to light, motion, and temperature.

Should either device flash or make a sound (e.g., a beeping noise)? +

No. Neither device will flash or make noise at any point during the study period.

What does the button on the light sensor do? +

Pressing this button will mark an "event" in your data. It does not need to be pressed at any point, but do not worry if it happens accidentally.

What will happen to my information? +

Within 3 months after you complete the study, your research data will be anonymised, meaning it will no longer be possible to identify you from the research data.
Your anonymised research data will be securely stored in Maynooth University servers for 10 years, in line with university research requirements. If you agree, your anonymised data will also be shared long term in an open science repository to support future scientific and methodological development work
