Light_NDD Research Study

Could you help us understand light sensitivity, sleep and wellbeing?

We are inviting adults aged 18–25 to take part in a research study at Maynooth University exploring light sensitivity, sleep and wellbeing in people with autism, ADHD or both, as well as adults without a neurodevelopmental condition.

Participant thank-you voucher €180

Email the Study Team

Could I take part?

You may be eligible to take part if you meet the following criteria:

  • Adults aged 18–25 · autistic, ADHD, or both (AuDHD)
    Diagnosis or self-identification is accepted · Low support needs (being able to consent and take part independently)
  • Adults aged 18–25 · without a neurodevelopmental condition

Who may not be eligible?
There are a few additional eligibility criteria to ensure that the study is safe and suitable for you, and that the measurements are valid and reliable. Please read the full criteria below, and contact the study team if you have questions about whether you may be eligible. You don't need to share private details.

  • Body Mass Index (BMI) below 18 or above 28
  • Pregnancy
  • Night shift work in the past 12 months
  • Recent travel across time zones (within the last 2 weeks)
  • Diagnosis of an intellectual disability
  • Presence (current or past) of schizophrenia or another psychotic disorder, or bipolar disorder
  • Diagnosis of a sleep breathing disorder, such as sleep apnoea
  • Diagnosis of a sleep breathing disorder, such as sleep apnoea
  • Diagnosis of narcolepsy
  • Presence of any ophthalmological or general medical condition affecting retinal/eye function such as cataract
  • Ocular disorders, colour blindness
  • Epilepsy or other major neurological diagnosis contraindicated for light exposure

Participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without giving a reason. Your data will be kept confidential. This study has been approved by XXX.
Further information about the study is provided in the Participant Information Sheet, which you will receive before deciding whether to take part. You can also view or download the PDF version below.

What will participation involve?

Evening visit

You will attend an evening research visit at Maynooth University where we will measure your pupil responses to light stimuli. This will take approximately 1 hour.

You will be offered transport home (taxi or public transport). If you prefer, a friend or family member can pick you up and is welcome to accompany you during the laboratory session if you wish.

Wear sensors for 2 weeks

You will wear a wrist actiwatch and a light sensor around your neck for 2 weeks.

Online questionnaires

You will complete online questionnaires about light, sleep, and mood.

Thank you for taking part

In recognition of your time, you will receive a €180 voucher and a graphical summary of your wearable data.

What taking part looks like?

Here is an overview of what you can expect when taking part in the Light_NDD study.

  1. Get in touch

    Online

    Contact the Study Team to ask questions or register your interest. light.ndd@mu.ie

  2. Read the study information

    Online

    We will email you the Participant Information Sheet and learn more about what the study involves.

    Your choice: You can decide not to continue at any point.

  3. Give informed consent

    Online

    If you would like to take part, you will provide informed consent before continuing.

  4. Complete the initial questionnaires

    Online ~20min

    Complete questions about your demographics, lifestyle, neurodevelopmental profile, and autistic and ADHD traits.

  5. Wear the light and sleep/activity sensors

    2 weeks

    Wear the actigraphy and light sensors for two weeks as part of your normal daily routine.

  6. Complete the main questionnaires

    Online ~60min

    Complete questionnaires about light, sleep, chronotype and mood.

  7. Evening session

    In the lab ~45min

    Attend an evening laboratory session where pupil measurements will be taken.
    Final debriefing questionnaire about the session and your experience of taking part in the study.

  8. Receive your incentive

    Online After the study

    Receive your €180 voucher and your individual wearable data summary.
