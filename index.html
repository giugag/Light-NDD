Light_NDD Research Study

We are looking for young adults aged 18–25 to help us understand how light sensitivity may impact sleep, circadian rhythms, and wellbeing in neurodevelopmental conditions.

Who can take part?

You may be eligible to take part if you meet the following criteria:

  • 18–25 years old
  • Autistic, ADHD, or AuDHD
    Diagnosis or self-identification is accepted · Low support needs
  • Neurotypical adults
    Without a neurodevelopmental condition

It is important that the study is safe and suitable for you. You will not be able to take part if any of the following apply:

  • Body Mass Index (BMI) below 18 or above 28 (calculated as BMI = weight (kg) / [height (m)]²)
  • Pregnancy
  • Night shift work in the past 12 months
  • Recent travel across time zones (within the last 2 months)
  • Diagnosis of an intellectual disability
  • Presence (current or past) of a psychotic disorder (e.g., bipolar disorder, schizophrenia)
  • Diagnosis of a sleep breathing disorder, such as sleep apnoea

Participation is voluntary. Further information about the study, including information about your rights as a participant, will be provided before you decide whether to take part.

What will participation involve?

Evening visit

You will attend an evening research visit at Maynooth University where we will measure your pupil responses to light stimuli. This will take approximately 1 hour.

You will be offered transport home (taxi or public transport). If you prefer, a friend or family member can pick you up and is welcome to accompany you during the laboratory session if you wish.

Wear sensors for 2 weeks

You will wear a wrist actiwatch and a light sensor around your neck for 2 weeks.

Online questionnaires

You will complete online questionnaires about light, sleep, and mood.

Thank you for taking part

In recognition of your time, you will receive a €180 voucher and a graphical summary of your wearable data.

Timeline of the study

Here is an overview of the steps involved in taking part in the Light_NDD study.

  1. Reply to study advertisement
  2. Read Participant Information Sheet
  3. Informed Consent
  4. Initial Online Questionnaires
  5. 2 weeks of actigraphy & light sensors
  6. Main Online Questionnaires (light, sleep, chronotype, mood)
  7. In-lab evening session: pupil measurement
  8. Debriefing
  9. Receive incentives
