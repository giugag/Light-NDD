Could you help us understand light sensitivity, sleep and wellbeing?

We are inviting adults aged 18–25 to take part in the Light_NDD research study at Maynooth University exploring light sensitivity, sleep and wellbeing in people with autism, ADHD or both, as well as adults without a neurodevelopmental condition.

Participant thank-you voucher €180 for completing the study

Email the Study Team

Could I take part?

You may be eligible to take part if you meet the following criteria:

  • Adults aged 18–25 · autistic, ADHD, or both (AuDHD)
    Diagnosis or self-identification accepted · Low support needs (being able to consent and take part independently)
  • Adults aged 18–25 · without a neurodevelopmental condition

Who may not be eligible?

There are a few additional eligibility criteria to ensure that the study is safe and suitable for you, and that the measurements are valid and reliable.

Please read the full criteria below, and contact the study team if you have questions. You don't need to share private details.

  • Body Mass Index (BMI) below 18 or above 28
  • Pregnancy
  • Night shift work in the past 12 months
  • Recent travel across time zones (within the last 2 weeks)
  • Diagnosis of an intellectual disability
  • Presence (current or past) of schizophrenia or another psychotic disorder, or bipolar disorder
  • Diagnosis of a sleep breathing disorder, such as sleep apnoea
  • Diagnosis of narcolepsy
  • Presence of any ophthalmological or general medical condition affecting retinal/eye function such as cataract
  • Ocular disorders, colour blindness
  • Epilepsy or other major neurological diagnosis contraindicated for light exposure

Participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time without giving a reason. Your data will be kept confidential.

What will taking part involve?

Taking part in the Light_NDD study involves: online questionnaires (about 1 hour 30 minutes in total), two weeks of wearing sensors, an evening visit to the lab (about 1 hour), and a thank-you voucher for completing all of them.

Here is what to expect, step by step.

  1. Get in touch

    Online

    Contact the Study Team to ask questions or register your interest. light.ndd@mu.ie

  2. Read the study information

    Online

    We'll email you the Participant Information Sheet so you can learn more about what the study involves.

    Your choice: You can decide not to continue at any point.

  3. Give informed consent

    Online

    If you would like to take part, you'll give your informed consent before continuing.

  4. Complete the initial questionnaires

    Online ~30min

    Questions about your demographics, lifestyle, neurodevelopmental profile, and autistic and ADHD traits.

  5. Wear the light and sleep/activity sensors

    2 weeks

    You'll wear a wrist actiwatch and a light sensor around your neck as part of your normal daily routine.

  6. Complete the main questionnaires

    Online ~1hour

    Questionnaires about light, sleep, chronotype and mood.

  7. Come to the evening lab visit

    Maynooth University ~1hour

    We’ll measure your pupil responses to light stimuli, then ask you to complete a short final questionnaire about your experience.
    We’ll offer you transport home (taxi or public transport), or a friend or family member can pick you up and join you in the lab if you prefer.

  8. Receive your thank-you

    Online After the study

    Once you’ve completed all the steps above, you’ll receive a €180 voucher and a graphical summary of your wearable data.
