Light_NDD Research Study

Take Part

We are looking for young adults aged 18–25 to help us understand how light sensitivity may relate to sleep, circadian rhythms and wellbeing.

Who can take part?

You may be eligible to take part if you meet the following criteria:

  • 18–25 years old
  • Autistic, have ADHD, or are AuDHD
  • Neurotypical adults without a neurodevelopmental condition

For participants with autism, ADHD or AuDHD, diagnosis or self-identification is accepted. The study is aimed at people with low support needs.

What will participation involve?

Evening visit

You will attend an evening research visit where we will measure your pupil responses to light stimuli. This will take approximately 1 hour.

Wear sensors for 2 weeks

You will wear a wrist ActiWatch and a light sensor around your neck for 2 weeks.

Online questionnaires

You will complete online questionnaires about your experiences, sleep and wellbeing.

Thank you for taking part

In recognition of your time, you will receive a €180 voucher and a graphical summary of your wearable data.

Participation is voluntary. Further information about the study, including information about your rights as a participant, will be provided before you decide whether to take part.
